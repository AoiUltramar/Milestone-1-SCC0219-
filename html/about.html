How it started

It was dark and perilous the time in which the evil warlocks (Sam Altman and Elon Musk) ruled the land using flying cars and automated chatbots. The team at cache and carry decided they had enough, the regular folk had to be able to fight them back using VM's. The battle is dangerous and the enemy has many eyes (Peter Thiel) so our heroes must also be armed with VPN's.

Today we run virtual machines in Brazilian data centers and a VPN with servers in twelve countries. Every new customer can try a Standard VM for free for 14 days.
