Servers and privacy, for people.

Cache & Carry sells virtual machines and VPN plans to students, freelancers and small teams who want their own corner of the internet without a cloud bill they can't read.

Our promise

Fixed monthly prices, no surprise charges, and a real person on the other end of every support ticket.

How it started

It began in a São Carlos lab, when a classmate needed a server for a weekend project and found only pricing pages that took longer to understand than the project itself. So we opened a shop with a short menu and honest prices.

Today we run virtual machines in Brazilian data centers and a VPN with servers in twelve countries. Every new customer can try a Standard VM for free for 14 days.
